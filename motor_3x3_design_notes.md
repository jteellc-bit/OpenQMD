# 3×3 Phase Coupled Motor — Synthetic Overview

## Geometry & Rationale
The 3×3 layout represents nine interaction channels between three stator and three rotor coils,
coupled through mutual inductance and cross-field dynamics.

### Simplifications
- Synthetic dataset mimics magnetic torque response via trigonometric relationships
- Noise and coupling terms emulate real inter-phase interference
