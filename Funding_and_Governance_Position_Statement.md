# Funding and Governance Position Statement
Quantum Motor Driver (QMD) Initiative
_Last updated: October 2025_

## Purpose and Principle
The QMD Initiative is dedicated to open, non-commercial research and the advancement of public knowledge in energy systems and dynamic efficiency.
Its goal is not to create capital instruments, development banks, or private markets, but to empower individuals and institutions with a transparent, reproducible, and ethical technological framework.

## Funding Stance
The project does not seek or manage financial flows.
QMD will remain insulated from direct capital streams to prevent financial exploitation, speculative capture, or monopolization.

Public or private development organizations may independently invest in QMD-related applications at their discretion. However, such efforts are external and shall not imply endorsement, affiliation, or governance over the QMD framework itself.

## Governance Model
The QMD Initiative shall be stewarded through open collaboration, version control transparency, and shared peer review rather than centralized authority.

All documentation, simulation tools, and specifications are released freely for public benefit under permissive open-source terms, subject only to ethical use.

## Closing Statement
By maintaining a separation between research integrity and financial interest, QMD ensures its mission remains clear: to serve as a freely available foundation for global efficiency, sustainability, and shared progress.
