# OpenQMD: Quantum Motor Driver Framework
Welcome to **OpenQMD**, an open-source initiative uniting quantum geometry, energy dynamics, and applied electromagnetism.

## Overview
OpenQMD extends the **Dirac Equation (DE) series** into practical quantum motor and generator architectures. It bridges theory and implementation via accessible simulation modules, experimental standards, and data repositories.
